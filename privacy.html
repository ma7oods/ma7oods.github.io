KeepSpot Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

This policy explains how the KeepSpot Android app ("KeepSpot", "the app") handles information. KeepSpot is a free, offline-first app for remembering where household items are stored. It is published by the developer named in the Contact section ("we", "us"). The app is free and shows one banner advertisement provided by Google AdMob.

Summary

  • Your places, things, and notes are stored only on your device. We do not receive, sell, or share them.
  • KeepSpot has no account, sign-in, or server of its own.
  • Core features work offline. Only the advertising feature uses the internet.
  • Google AdMob shows one banner ad and collects some device data to do so. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first and can change your choice later in the app.
  • Backup files are created only when you choose to export one, and you decide where they go.

Information stored on your device

The names of places and things you enter, your notes, and your app settings (such as your language choice) are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. This information is not sent to us, to Google AdMob, or to anyone else by the app. Your ad consent choice is also stored on your device by Google's consent software. We cannot see it, recover it, or delete it for you. Please avoid entering highly sensitive details, such as passwords or bank information, in item names or notes.

Android automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for KeepSpot, so your list is not copied to a cloud backup by Android.

No account

KeepSpot does not ask you to create an account or sign in, and it does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Backup files

When you choose Export backup, the app creates a JSON file containing your places, things, and notes and opens your device's share options. You choose where the file goes, for example a file manager, a cloud drive, or a message to yourself. Once you share the file, it is handled by the app or service you chose, under that service's own terms and privacy policy. When you choose Restore backup, the app reads the file you select and, after you confirm, replaces the list on your device. Backup files are not encrypted, so store them somewhere you trust.

Advertising with Google AdMob

KeepSpot shows one banner ad at the bottom of the screen using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob), provided by Google LLC. To show ads, the SDK needs an internet connection and automatically collects and shares some information with Google. According to Google, this can include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general (approximate) location. KeepSpot does not request the Android location permission.
  • Device and advertising identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.
  • Ad interactions, such as ad views, ad taps, and app launches.
  • Diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the SDK, such as launch time, hangs, and energy use.
  • Device information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size, and network type.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure ad performance, produce aggregate reporting, and prevent fraud and abuse. Google encrypts this data in transit. Where consent is required, ads are personalized only if you consent; otherwise Google may show non-personalized or limited ads, which still use some information, such as your IP address, to deliver the ad, measure it, and prevent fraud. None of your places, things, or notes are given to Google AdMob.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Google processes this data as described in those policies and may process it in the United States and other countries.

Your consent and privacy choices

KeepSpot uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent where the law requires it. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you will see a consent message from Google before personalized ads are shown and before information is stored on or read from your device for advertising. Where US state privacy laws apply, a message may let you opt out of the use of your information for targeted advertising.

To change your choices at any time: open KeepSpot, go to the More screen, and tap Privacy choices to review or change the choice you made in the consent or opt-out message that applies in your region.

You can also control advertising on your device:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or limit ad personalization, in your Android settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device).
  • Manage Google ad personalization at My Ad Center.

Permissions

The Google Mobile Ads SDK adds the permissions it needs to load and measure ads: internet and network-state access, the advertising ID permission, Android's ad services (Privacy Sandbox) permissions for the advertising ID, attribution, and Topics APIs, which the SDK may use on devices that support them, and wake-lock and foreground-service permissions for its background work. KeepSpot does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, or storage permissions. The Android file picker and share sheet are used only when you choose backup actions.

Children

KeepSpot is a general household tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. KeepSpot itself does not collect personal information from any user; advertising data is handled by Google as described above. If you believe a child under 13 is using the app, contact us using the details below.

Data retention

Your places, things, notes, and settings remain on your device until you delete them in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. We do not keep any copy. Backup files you exported remain wherever you saved or sent them until you delete them there. Information collected by Google for advertising is retained by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting your information

  • In the app: on the More screen, use Delete saved entries to remove all places, things, notes, and temporary in-app backup files from the device.
  • Clear data or uninstall: clearing KeepSpot's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app, removes everything the app stored on the device.
  • Exported backups: delete those files from the app, folder, or service where you saved them.
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings and use the Google controls linked above. Because we do not receive this data, we cannot delete it for you.

Security

Your list is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot normally read. Advertising data is sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so keep your device locked and store exported backups somewhere safe.
