Privacy Policy

yutuMa7oods

Effective date: 14 November 2026

yutuMa7oods ("the tool") is a private desktop tool published by Balbol ("we", "us"). Its owner uses it to manage his own YouTube channels. It is described on its home page. The tool is not offered to the public and has no users other than its owner.

Google user data the tool accesses

The tool signs in with Google (OAuth 2.0) and asks for these YouTube permissions on the channel the owner picks:

  • Manage the YouTube account: see, upload and edit the channel's videos, thumbnails and playlists.
  • See the channel's public and private details: its name, its video list, and counts such as views, likes and subscribers.
  • See the list of the channel's current members, if it has any.

The tool does not read the owner's Google password, email messages, contacts, files or any data from other Google products.

How the data is used

Only to carry out what the owner asks for on his own channels: upload a video, change a video's title, description, tags or thumbnail, manage playlists, and show the channel's numbers. The data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to build profiles of anyone.

Where the data is stored

The tool has no server and no database. Requests go directly from the owner's computer to Google. The sign-in token that Google issues is kept in a file on that computer. Channel details and numbers that the tool reads are shown to the owner and may be kept in his own working notes on the same computer.

Sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer Google user data to anyone. The owner may run the tool with automation or AI assistant software on his computer; in that case the details the tool returns are processed by that software on his behalf, only to complete the task he asked for.

Keeping and deleting the data

The sign-in token stays on the owner's computer until he deletes the file or removes the tool's access. Access can be removed at any time in the Google Account, under Third-party apps and services. Removing access makes the stored token useless. Working notes are deleted by deleting the files.

Google and YouTube policies

The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The tool uses YouTube API Services. Use of the tool is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The tool is not directed to children and is not used by them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
